Otterly Privacy Notice

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Otterly is published by Mj Alvear, an individual developer in the Philippines. Questions: mjalvear44@gmail.com.

  • We collect nothing. No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Otterly never connects to the internet (on Android it has no internet permission). We never see, receive or share your entries.
  • Everything stays on your phone. Your entries, moods and feelings, notebooks and Ollie's replies are kept in an encrypted database; small settings (like your theme and reminder time) are kept in the app's private storage. The key stays on your device. Otterly marks the journal to be excluded from phone backups and keeps the key on this device only, so export before changing phones.
  • On-device AI. Ollie's replies come from an on-device model (based on Google's Gemma) and a feelings classifier, both on your phone. Replies can be wrong. You can report or hide a reply; reporting opens your own email app and sends only what you choose to send.
  • Phone permissions. Notifications for the daily reminder (with alarms and restart-after-reboot so it arrives on time), a motion sensor for the tilting jar, the share sheet when you export, and the dialer when you tap a support line. No contacts, location, camera, microphone or photos.
  • Delete. Me → Settings → Delete everything, or uninstalling, erases your data. Export is yours; the exported file is not encrypted.
  • Your rights. Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act you can access, correct and erase your data. Since it all stays on your phone, you do this in the app (edit, export, Delete everything); for questions, email us.
  • Age. Otterly is for people 13 and older. If you are 13 to 17, use it with a parent or guardian aware.
  • Not medical advice. Otterly is not a medical or crisis service. Crisis lines for the Philippines are in the app under Me → Talk to someone. In an emergency, call 911.
  • Changes. Updates are posted on this page with a new date.
